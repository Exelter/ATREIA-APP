What Atreia App does

Atreia App shows overlays over AION 2: your damage meter, boss timers and mechanics, pets, loot and more. It reads the game's network traffic on this PC to know what happens in your fights. It never changes the game, its files or its memory, and it never sends anything to the game.

What it needs

Npcap, the free network capture driver, so the app can read the game's traffic. If it isn't on this PC yet, setup offers to open its download page at the end, and the app shows you the way too.

Where it goes

The app installs for your Windows account only, in your user folder (AppData\Local\AtreiaApp). It doesn't need administrator rights. It puts a shortcut on your desktop and in the Start menu.

Updates

The app updates itself from atreia.app and shows what's new in each update.

Your data

Your fights stay on this PC unless you upload them to atreia.app: by hand, or on their own if you turn that on. Signing in with Discord is optional.

Removing it

Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Atreia App > Uninstall.
